# Sample Debrief Script (Facilitator)

**Timebox:** 30 minutes  
**Facilitator:** HM or Tech HR  
**Rule:** No persuasion before independent votes are on the table.

---

## Agenda

1. **Silent recap (2m)** — each interviewer opens their scorecard  
2. **Independent votes (3m)** — go-around: vote + level + one sentence  
3. **Evidence (15m)** — round by round; challenge vague praise  
4. **Level debate (5m)** — Senior vs Staff explicitly  
5. **Comp (3m)** — ₹80L yes/no with risks  
6. **Decision (2m)** — offer / hold / reject + owner for next step  

---

## Facilitator prompts

- “What did you hear that was *Staff-scoped*, not Senior-scoped?”
- “Where did they show security judgment under uncertainty?”
- “What’s the biggest risk if we hire them tomorrow?”
- “Would you trust them alone on a sev-1 at 2am in month two?”

---

## Recording template

```text
Candidate:
Votes: 
Level consensus:
₹80L Staff: YES / NO
Decision: OFFER / HOLD / REJECT
Hold reason / next round:
Offer owner:
Ramp risks:
```

---

## Anti-patterns in debrief

- Highest title in room dominates
- “I liked them” without evidence
- Comp urgency overriding level
- Averaging a NO on security with STRONG on coding into HIRE
