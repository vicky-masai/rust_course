# Staff Software Development Engineer at Zscaler

Use this page to keep the interview aligned to the **actual job**, not a generic Staff title.

---

## Job title (use everywhere)

**Staff Software Development Engineer — Zscaler**

Also acceptable short form in notes: **Staff SDE (Zscaler)**

Do **not** interview as if this were:
- Engineering Manager
- Solutions Architect only
- Junior/Mid SDE
- Generic “fullstack CRUD” Staff

---

## Mission of the role

Design, build, and operate software that powers Zscaler’s **cloud security platform**: high concurrency, low latency, multi-tenant isolation, strong auditability, and security-first failure modes.

Typical problem spaces (candidate need not have all; should map experience to at least two):
- Data-plane / proxy / gateway / connector style systems
- Control-plane APIs, policy distribution, config rollout
- Identity-aware access / zero-trust style flows
- High-volume logging, telemetry, or policy evaluation
- Platform libraries, performance, reliability for other SDEs

Primary language on this candidate’s resume: **Rust** → verify production systems depth.

## Level check: Senior SDE vs Staff SDE

| Senior SDE | Staff SDE (this role) |
|------------|------------------------|
| Owns a service or large features | Owns a **problem domain** / critical path |
| Strong executor in one team | Multiplies **multiple teams** |
| Designs within architecture | Sets / evolves architecture with trade-offs |
| Mentors informally | Raises hiring + engineering bar deliberately |
| Good at delivery | Good at delivery **and** org technical outcomes |

**₹80L Staff SDE offer** requires Staff column evidence — not Senior delivery alone.
