# Round: Behavioral & Culture — Q&A

**Duration:** 30–45m  
**Method:** STAR, but push for metrics, names of decisions, and failures.

---

## Ownership & incidents

### B1. Tell me about the highest-severity incident you owned.

**Good**
- Detection → mitigation → root cause → prevention; customer impact honesty; personal role clear; postmortem blameless but accountable.

**Probe:** What would you do differently? What systemic fix landed?

---

### B2. A deploy you made caused customer impact. Walk through it.

**Good**
- Ownership without self-flagellation theater; rollback discipline; feature flags; improved tests/canaries after.

**Red flag:** Blames CI, “bad luck,” or teammates exclusively.

---

## Collaboration

### B3. Conflict with a peer on priority or design — resolution?

**Good**
- Data, customer impact, experiment; involves manager appropriately; relationship intact.

---

### B4. Working with Product / Support / Sales engineering under pressure.

**Good**
- Translates technical risk to business language; protects security invariants; finds pragmatic path.

---

## Craft & quality

### B5. Time you stopped a dangerous change from shipping.

**Good**
- Concrete risk (authz, data leak, irreversible migration); escalation path; how they built allies.

---

### B6. Feedback you received that changed your behavior.

**Good**
- Specific, non-defensive, lasting change.

---

## Motivation & ethics

### B7. Why cloud security / Zscaler?

**Good**
- Substance (threat landscape, scale, product curiosity); not only brand/salary.

---

### B8. Ethical gray area (user data, shortcut past review). What did you do?

**Good**
- Protects customers; refuses silent security bypass; uses proper exception process.

**Hard no:** Proud of bypassing controls to ship faster.

---

## Work style

### B9. How do you manage interrupt-driven work (on-call + projects)?

**Good**
- Time boxing, error budgets, documenting, saying no, aligning manager on priorities.

---

### B10. Diversity of collaboration: remote / cross-geo / junior partners.

**Good**
- Inclusive practices; async docs; timezone respect.

---

## Light Tech HR checks (screen or close)

- Notice period, joining constraints
- Competing offers (do not pressure illegally; gather timeline)
- Willingness for on-call / customer escalations
- Relocation / hybrid expectations
- Background: can they work with enterprise security customers?

---

## Scoring keys

| Score | Evidence |
|-------|----------|
| 4 | Multiple strong stories with impact + learning |
| 3 | Solid ownership; some depth missing |
| 2 | Generic stories; unclear personal impact |
| 1 | Blame-heavy, unethical, or fabricated-feeling |
