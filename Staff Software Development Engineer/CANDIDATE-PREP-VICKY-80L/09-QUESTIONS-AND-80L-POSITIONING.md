# 06 — Your Questions + ₹80L Positioning

## Questions to ask the Senior Staff interviewer (pick 2–3)

Technical hour — show Staff curiosity:

1. “What problem domain would this Staff SDE own in the first two quarters — data plane, control plane, or platform?”  
2. “What does a great Staff SDE look like here at month six — what metrics or outcomes?”  
3. “Where do you feel the most pain today: reliability, policy correctness, performance, or developer leverage?”  
4. “How do you handle fail-open vs fail-closed decisions across product and engineering?”  
5. “What’s the Rust (or systems language) footprint on your team, and where is FFI/legacy still involved?”  

Avoid in this round:
- Exact ₹80L ask (unless they open comp)
- “How soon until promotion?”
- Gossip about competitors

---

## Comp framing (when HR / HM asks expectation)

**Principle:** Anchor to Staff scope + market, stay flexible, don’t invent competing offers.

### Strong line
> “I’m interviewing for **Staff Software Development Engineer**. Given the scope — ownership of security-critical cloud systems, on-call, and Staff-level leverage — I’m targeting a total package in the **Staff band around ₹80 Lakh CTC**, depending on equity/bonus structure and leveling. I’m flexible on mix if the scope is truly Staff.”

### If they say you look Senior
> “I’m open to discussing level with evidence from this loop. If the consensus is Senior SDE, we should align title and comp to that band honestly — I don’t want a mismatched Staff title. If feedback is Staff-scope ownership, I’d like the package to match.”

### If pressed for a single number early
> “For Staff SDE at Zscaler scope, my expectation centers near **₹80L CTC**; happy to calibrate after leveling feedback.”

## How HR screen differs from Staff technical hour

| HR / Tech recruiter | Senior Staff engineer (your 60 min) |
|---------------------|-------------------------------------|
| Notice, location, comp range, motivation | Deep systems + Rust + design + security |
| “Why Zscaler?” | “Prove you can own blast-radius software” |
| Culture / logistics | Scorecard toward Offer Staff / Senior / Reject |

**Why Zscaler (30–45 sec):**
> “I want to apply multi-tenant systems and Rust production work to cloud security at global scale. Zscaler’s data-plane/control-plane problems — policy, isolation, reliability — match how I already think about invariants and failure modes. I’m looking for Staff-scope ownership, not just feature tickets.”

## Closing mental model

You clear **Staff Software Development Engineer @ Zscaler ~₹80L** when the interviewer believes:

1. You **build** (SDE), not only advise  
2. You own **invariants** under failure  
3. You think in **tenant + security** terms  
4. You can grow into **edge scale** even if WMS QPS is smaller  
5. You multiply others via **contracts and measured migrations**

Your resume gets you the interview.  
These scripts get you the score.  
HR process + leveling still finalize the written offer.
