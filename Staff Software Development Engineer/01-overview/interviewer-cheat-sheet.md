# Interviewer Cheat Sheet (1-page)

Print or keep open during the interview.

## Opening (2 min)

- Introduce role + your background
- Agenda + “think aloud; ask clarifying questions”
- Confirm Rust production context briefly

## Time boxing

| Segment | Minutes |
|---------|---------|
| Deep technical probes | 35–45 |
| Coding or design spike | 20–30 |
| Candidate questions | 5–10 |

## Universal probes (use anytime)

1. “What fails first at 10× traffic?”
2. “How does tenant B get isolated from tenant A?”
3. “What do you measure in production for this?”
4. “Fail open or fail closed — why?”
5. “What was *your* decision vs the team’s?”
6. “How would you roll this back?”

## Evidence verbs (write these down)

Prefer notes like: *identified*, *measured*, *rolled back*, *wrote RFC*, *reduced p99*, *blocked ship*, *mentored*, *owned sev-1*.

Avoid: *seems smart*, *good vibes*, *N/A*.

## Staff vs Senior (in-room test)

Ask: “Who adopted your change outside your team, and how do you know?”

- Names + metrics → Staff signal  
- “My PR was praised” → Senior signal  

## Closing

- Candidate questions
- Do **not** give hire signal
- Same-day scorecard mandatory

## Confidentiality

Do not share expected answers, other candidates’ performance, or ₹80L internal bar with the candidate.
