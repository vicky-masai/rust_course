# Hiring Bar — Staff Software Development Engineer at Zscaler (₹80L)

## Role summary (what we hire for)

**Staff Software Development Engineer (Staff SDE) at Zscaler** is not “senior who codes faster.” It is:

1. **Technical depth** in systems, networking, and security-adjacent software
2. **Breadth** across services, platforms, and operational reality
3. **Leverage** — multiplies other engineers via design, standards, reviews
4. **Judgment** under ambiguity and incident pressure
5. **Trust** with customer traffic, policy, and sensitive data paths

Primary language on resume: **Rust**. We still expect fluency in distributed systems, APIs, observability, and cloud operations — Rust alone is not enough.

---

## ₹80 Lakh bar (what must be true)

Treat ₹80L as **strong Staff / top Senior-borderline Staff** in India market for cloud security. Candidate must clear:

| Dimension | Minimum for offer |
|-----------|-------------------|
| Rust production depth | Can design memory-safe high-throughput services; knows unsafe boundaries |
| System design | Designs multi-tenant, failure-aware, security-aware systems |
| Distributed systems | CAP, consistency, retries, idempotency, backpressure — applied, not buzzwords |
| Security mindset | Threat models, least privilege, data classification, abuse cases |
| Staff behaviors | Cross-team influence, technical strategy, mentoring, conflict navigation |
| Communication | Clear, structured, evidence-based; no hand-waving |
| Level | Consistently Staff signals in ≥3 technical rounds |

**Soft rule:** If coding is excellent but leadership/scope is Senior, offer Senior band — not ₹80L Staff. If leadership is strong but Rust/systems shallow, do not stretch for Staff title.

---

## Level differentiation

### Senior (do not offer Staff / ₹80L as Staff)

- Owns a service or large feature end-to-end
- Strong executor; occasional design docs
- Mentors juniors informally
- Designs within existing architecture

### Staff (target)

- Owns a **problem domain** (e.g., policy evaluation path, connector reliability, data plane perf)
- Sets technical direction across 2+ teams
- Anticipates scale, abuse, and operational cost
- Raises quality bar (reviews, standards, platforms)
- Makes hard trade-offs explicit and documented

### Principal (overqualified / different loop)

- Company-wide technical strategy
- Invents new product/platform bets
- External industry impact

---

## Zscaler-context expectations

Candidate should show comfort with at least **two** of:

- Cloud security / zero trust / proxy / gateway concepts
- High-scale networking (TCP/TLS, connection pools, timeouts)
- Multi-tenant SaaS control plane + data plane
- Policy engines, identity, authentication/authorization
- Observability and SRE practices for always-on services

They need not have worked at Zscaler. They must map their experience to our constraints when prompted.

---

## Anti-patterns (automatic caution)

- “I only do happy-path features; ops is someone else’s job”
- Cannot explain a production incident they owned
- Resume lists Rust but interview is only CRUD + frameworks
- Designs that ignore tenant isolation or data leakage
- Staff claim with zero evidence of influence beyond their squad

---

## Pass philosophy

Prefer **false reject over false hire** at Staff + ₹80L. A wrong Staff hire taxes every team for years.
